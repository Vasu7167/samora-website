The flow

From a list of companies to booked meetings.

01

Find the people

SAM maps who decides at each company, ranked by the roles you set for this SAMpaign. Only people who match are revealed and paid for.

02

Write to each one

One genuinely different message per person, from their role and what is actually happening at their company. Never the same email with the name swapped.

03

Check before it leaves

Every email passes the send check: the right name, no empty tags, no notes meant for the writer, and an address SAM trusts.

04

Send from your own mailbox and LinkedIn

At a pace that protects your domain. LinkedIn visits, invites and messages run through Samora for LinkedIn, in your browser.

05

Answer what comes back

Replies, out-of-office notes and meetings return on their own. Let SAM draft the answer in the language they wrote in, and you send it.

Languages

Any language, with the English beside it.

Set a language for the SAMpaign, or let SAM pick each person’s own. Every email, note, message and answer comes with an English version for you.

  • Thai, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Melayu, Vietnamese, French, Spanish, German and English
  • Change the English and SAM carries it into the original
  • Outside English, every message waits for your approval
  • Thai polite endings are your choice, never guessed
