The Mechanism

Why the reply rate moves.

A standalone sequencer is blind to your pipeline, so it writes from a template and a first name. SAMpaigns are anchored to the account: every send is informed by the deal history, and every reply returns as verified signal. That is the only lever in the chain above that software can actually pull.
