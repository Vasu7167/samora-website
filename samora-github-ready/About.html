What 12 years taught him
01
GTM fails at the data layer first

Not strategy. Not people. The data that drives decisions is wrong from the start. because it was entered by a human trying to avoid a CRM update.

02
Reps are not the problem

They are trapped in a system designed to punish them for doing real work instead of data entry. Fix the verification layer. The rep's work becomes the data.

03
New regions fail predictably

No ICP. No honest pipeline. No feedback loop. The playbook lands but the signals never come back. Region auto-mapping and external intelligence fix this structurally.

04
The real bottleneck is unverified claims

Deals die in the gap between what was logged and what actually happened. Three-tier verification closes that gap permanently.

05
The next decade belongs to signal infrastructure

AI can only be as good as the signals it reads. Build the verification layer first. Everything. forecast, coaching, strategy. follows from honest data.

The signals were always there.

Nobody made them speak to each other.

Every email your rep sent. Every call they took. Every note they scribbled after a meeting. Every priority they logged at the start of the day. Every completion note that said "budget confirmed." Every LinkedIn message. Every WhatsApp reply. Every calendar event. Every step your sequencing tool fired. Every external signal about your prospect's company.

All of it existed. All of it was unstructured, siloed, speaking to nobody. The rep didn't know if they were doing enough. The manager didn't know what feedback to give beyond "do more." The board was looking at a forecast built on hope. and a CRM built on compliance, not truth.

Samora takes all of that unstructured reality. from every source, every channel, every human in your revenue team and every tool they use. and makes it structured, verified and intelligent. Three-tier pipeline classification. Signal-weighted probability. Region-mapped. Product-level. Role-specific. And then Agent SAM starts speaking. To the rep who needs clarity. To the manager who needs to coach. To the board who deserves the honest number.

This is not a better CRM. This is what comes after the CRM. Verified intelligence infrastructure built on one conviction: honesty at the data layer is a revenue strategy. and the dividend belongs to everyone. the SDR, the AE, the manager, the revenue leader and the board.
