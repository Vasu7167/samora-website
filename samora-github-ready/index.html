SamoraTrack

Your personal deal
management coach.
Not a tracker.

Most reps end the week not knowing if they had a good week. They did a lot. But did the right things move? SamoraTrack gives every salesperson a simple daily framework to get their day right, their week right, their month right. The signals are a byproduct. The clarity is the product.

This is your own personal compass. Log your top 3 priorities at the start of the day. Close them before noon. Log what blocked you. Log what you won. Get a structured review at end of day that tells you exactly what to do differently tomorrow. That is the whole habit.
