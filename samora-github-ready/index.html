From a stranger’s profile to a booked conversation.

    SAM visits the profile

    Through Samora for LinkedIn, in your own browser, like a person would. Within LinkedIn’s limits.

    How they communicate, what they care about right now, the objection they are likely to raise and the best way in.

    A note worth accepting

    Written after the visit, in your voice and in their language. Checked so it never reads like a tool wrote it.

  4. 04
    The first message on accept

    Picks up where the note left off. Interest first, never a pitch in the first touch.

  5. 05
    SAM reads their reply

    Interested, a question, not now, wrong person or away. SAM tells you what it means and what to do.

  6. 06
    SAM drafts the answer

    In their language, in their own conversation, with the English beside it. You read it and press send.
