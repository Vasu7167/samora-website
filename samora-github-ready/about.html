Samora/About

Forty countries. Eighty markets. One map.

Samora was not built in a boardroom. It was built by someone who spent twelve years opening markets the hard way, and got tired of doing it without a map.

Vasu Bansal, Founder and Builder-in-Chief

The path

Where the thesis came from.

2014 to 2016

Accenture

Building and gathering requirements for enterprise applications. Clients never wanted technology for its own sake. They wanted to see clearly today and decide well tomorrow.

2018 to 2020

Global expansion at a Fortune 500

An MBA added the business lens. Opening new logos and new geographies was the thrill. Owning a region with a disciplined MEDDPICC practice was the training.

2021 to 2026

Enterprise SaaS, from zero to scale

Leading EMEA from the ground up, building partner ecosystems across emerging economies, and more than 100 enterprise deals. The same problem every quarter: a CRM nobody trusted, and new regions entered without a map.

2026

Samora Global

Ten months in stealth, building with design partners. Launched in October 2026. Alongside it, GTM advice to early-stage startups taking their products into new markets.

What the work taught

Five lessons, built into the product.

01

Every market has its own trust threshold

Who decides, how they like to be approached, what they need to see first. You learn it fast or you do not get in.

02

The product is rarely the bottleneck

Getting it into the right rooms is. That is a finding, reaching and writing problem, which software can solve.

03

GTM fails at the data first

Not strategy, not people. The numbers driving decisions were typed by someone avoiding a CRM update.

04

Reps are not the problem

They are trapped in systems that reward data entry over selling. Make the work the data and they get their day back.

05

Language is respect

A buyer in Bangkok or Munich answers someone who wrote to them properly, in their language.
